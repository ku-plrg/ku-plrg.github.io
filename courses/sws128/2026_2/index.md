### SWS128: Software Analysis, 2026 Fall

The primary goal of this course is to learn the **theory** and **implementation**
of **software analysis**, both **static** and **dynamic**.

#### Grading

- **3 Homework Assignments: 90%**
  - 30% each, submitted on the [LMS](https://lms.korea.ac.kr/).
  - Each is assigned right after the lecture that covers it and is due **two
    weeks** later.
- **Attendance: 10%**
  - **All or nothing:** you get the full **10%** if you attend at least **2/3**
    of the checks, and **0%** otherwise.
  - There is **no attendance check** in the first week; it starts on
    **September 14**.

There is **no midterm exam, no final exam, and no term project**.
